A partner says riders are complaining that buses shown in the app are not arriving.
What would you do before deciding what needs to change?
Checkpoints
  • Clarify what riders are actually seeing, where, when and how often.
  • Separate scheduled information, real-time information, operational cancellations, processing and what the interface communicates.
  • Look for both quantitative and qualitative evidence.
  • Narrow the likely cause before deciding on a change.
  • Make ownership and next steps clear.
  • Decide what evidence would show that the problem is resolved.
