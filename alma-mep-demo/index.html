Try the kitchen demo

You can click through the entire example without changing any real records. All figures are made up for demonstration.

1
M.E.P. · Work through the listTap Done when something is prepared, or Stocked when there's enough from an earlier batch. Use Edit to rename or add notes.
2
Groceries · Add what needs buyingType a specific ingredient, optional quantity, and check it off when purchased. You can also flag groceries from a prep task.
3
Stock · Record what's on handUse the sample stock counts and edit a quantity. A low-stock tag appears if the count is below the target you set.
4
Reservations · Manage tables and guestsOn M.E.P., add or edit a table reservation. Enter total guests plus optional 5-course and 9-course counts. Undecided guests stay unassigned. Try the sample four-person reservation to see prep estimates update.
5
Day overview · See what a manager might seePreview progress, open purchases, stock alerts and changes in one place. Role-based manager access is not built yet.
6
Reset whenever you likePress Reset demo to restore the fictional sample data, or export JSON to keep a copy of your edits.
